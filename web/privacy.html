Privacy

There is no server to leak.

Cadence has no backend, no user accounts, no analytics and no third-party services. This isn't a policy promise on top of a data pipeline — there is no pipeline. Everything below can be checked against the source.

No account

Nothing to sign up for, nothing to sign in to, no email address collected, no password to lose.

No analytics

No telemetry, crash reporting, ad SDK, tag manager or fingerprinting — in either app or on this website.

No sync

Your log is never uploaded anywhere. Moving it between devices is something you do deliberately, with a file.

Where your data actually lives

One place: the device you typed it on.

  • Web app — your browser's IndexedDB storage for this origin, plus a small amount of local settings state. It is scoped to the browser profile, so a different browser or a private window is a different, empty logbook.
  • iOS app — the app's own on-device SwiftData store, inside the app's sandbox and covered by the device's own encryption.

That store holds what you'd expect: sessions and their sets, programs and their mid-cycle state, tracked lifts, your exercise library and edits to it, gyms and their plate inventories, bodyweight entries, body-signal flags and check-ins, milestones, and your settings.

What leaves your device

Almost nothing, and nothing about your training. To be precise:

ThingWhat happens
Your training data Never transmitted. There is no endpoint to transmit it to.
Web app files Downloaded from GitHub Pages the first time, then cached by a service worker so the app opens with no signal. The app makes no other network requests — no fonts, no CDNs, no APIs.
Your IP address Like any website, fetching these files means GitHub's servers see the request. That's between you and GitHub, and it's the only reason this site touches a network at all. Once the app is cached, it stops needing to.
Apple Health (iOS, optional) Two separate permissions, both off by default and granted independently. Writing sends completed workouts and bodyweight to Health. Comparing reads walking, running, and cycling distance — only for a session you have already logged, never continuously or in the background — and shows it beside your own number so you can decide which to keep. Cadence never changes a logged workout on its own, and reads nothing else: not heart rate, not sleep, not weight. Health data stays on your device under Apple's control, not Cadence's.
Notifications Scheduled locally on the device. No push server, so no server knows when you train.
Backups you export A file you save wherever you choose. If you put it in a cloud drive, it's in a cloud drive — that's your call, and worth making consciously.

Cookies

None. This site sets no cookies and the app sets no cookies. The app does use on-device storage (IndexedDB and the service-worker cache) to be your logbook and to work offline — that's local storage, not tracking, and it never leaves the device.

Your gym membership barcode

If you photograph a membership barcode, that image is stored in the same local store as everything else and displayed only by you, on your screen. One deliberate safety rule: on import, barcode images are accepted only as inline image data and never as a remote URL — so a tampered backup file can't turn the app into something that phones home.

The repository contains none of this

The exercise library, program templates and anatomy data that ship with Cadence are generic reference material. No workout history, bodyweight, health signal, program state, gym membership detail or personal starting weight exists anywhere in the source. That's enforced by the project's own contribution rules, not just good intentions.

Deleting everything

  • In the app: Settings → Reset all data wipes the device's copy. The generic exercise library and a default gym come back on next launch; your history does not.
  • Web app: clearing site data for this origin, or removing the installed app, removes the store and the offline cache with it.
  • iOS app: deleting the app removes its sandbox, store and all. Anything already written to Apple Health is managed in the Health app, not by Cadence.

There's no "delete my account" request to file, because there's no account and no copy of your data anywhere else. The corollary is the important part: if you delete it and have no exported backup, it's gone. Keep a backup.

Security reports

Found something? The project's security policy explains how to report it. Please don't include real training or health data in a report.

How to verify any of this. The web app is unminified, unbundled JavaScript — open your browser's network tab and watch it make no requests. The iOS app's source, its capabilities and its entitlements are all in the repository.
