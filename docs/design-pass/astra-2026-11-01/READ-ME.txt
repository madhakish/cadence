Generation adapts the supplied gorilla and can change individual strokes, anatomy, lettering and hardware details. For production use the unchanged original source artwork with a real material/decal transform; do not replace the canonical anatomy image or masks with generated redraws. UI AND MATERIAL DIRECTION
- Achieved load and next action lead; secondary labels stay quiet.
- Session mockup synthetic data: 225 lb = 45 lb bar + four 45 lb discs, 102.1 kg displayed.
- Loading mockup synthetic data: 135 lb = 45 lb bar + two 45 lb discs, 61.2 kg displayed. Training collars are visually represented without an added competition-collar mass.
- Corrected reference row in 01 reads 55 / 45 / 35 / 25 / 10 LB; this is a visual training-bumper scheme, not an IWF kilogram guide.
- Material distinctions: tight bright chrome reflections; fine zoned shaft knurl; smooth sleeve surfaces; matte rubber with molded edges; thinner calibrated steel; rough raised cast iron.
- Both stacks land on rubber, wood grain continues through the burn, and the platform has soft contact shadows.
- Logo is an integrated material treatment. Background stays quiet enough that the equipment and controls are readable.
